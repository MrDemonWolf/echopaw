Use the native offline preview on Apple Silicon with macOS 15 or later. [Build it from source](/develop/installation/) or try the [browser interface preview](/start/interface-preview/).

<SetupChecklist />

1. Open EchoPaw. The mascot preview is local and needs no account or microphone permission. Practice off-stream because a configured OBS scene can still capture the visible mascot or test sound.
2. Use the sample or choose a folder with your [two images](/character/images/). If a file is missing or mismatched, fix it and choose the folder again.
3. Run **Test output** at a low volume and watch the mascot change poses. If it is silent, check your output device and the app's volume.
4. Try **Emergency stop** during playback. Choose **Resume preview** and unmute before you play the next test.

For a stream, continue with [OBS](/stream/obs/) or choose individual steps in [Advanced Setup](/start/advanced-setup/).
