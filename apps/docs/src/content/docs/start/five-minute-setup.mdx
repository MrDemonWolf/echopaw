[Build EchoPaw from source](/develop/installation/) on an Apple Silicon Mac. You can rehearse with the starter wolf without accounts or microphone access.

## Try the sample

1. Open **Setup Guide** from the sidebar or Help menu. Choose your audio output and select **Play sample line** to hear the wolf.
2. Continue to the OBS step and record a short test scene. Confirm that you see the wolf and hear its line once. You can skip this step and return later.
3. Finish the guide or choose **Open API Keys settings** to jump to **API Keys** in the main window. You can run the rehearsal entirely offline.

Use **Character > Preview blink** to see the closed-eye pose on demand. **Use starter wolf** in the inspector restores the sample after a custom import. Adjust the volume and audio device in [Audio & capture](/stream/audio/).

## Mute and stop

**Mute speech** ends playback. Automatic blinking and optional microphone listening have their own controls. The preview stays local even after you [connect Twitch](/connect/twitch-broadcaster/) or [save an API key](/connect/openai/).

**Emergency stop** stops sound and listening and closes the capture panel. **Resume preview** restores controls. Choose **Unmute speech** and reopen the capture window yourself when ready.
