---
title: Keyword listening
---

Under **Settings** in the sidebar, select **Microphone**. Turn on **Detect a wake phrase on this Mac** and enter your phrase. **React to my voice** independently controls mouth movement. Choose **Start listening** and allow the microphone and Speech Recognition permission prompts.

A match shows **Wake phrase heard locally** on the **Microphone** page. It cannot generate or send a response in the offline build. EchoPaw requires an available on-device recognizer for the current language. If the recognizer is unavailable, turn off phrase detection to use simple voice activity. If recognition ends, the app releases the mic and shows how to restart it.

**Stop listening** and **Emergency stop** both release the microphone. The app saves the configured phrase and input preference. It keeps no microphone recordings or transcript history.
