---
title: Connect a separate Twitch bot
---

Use a Twitch account you control for chat automation. EchoPaw will show the bot and broadcaster identities side by side.

1. Choose **Connect bot** and approve access as the bot account. Check the account shown on the Twitch approval screen.
2. Enable incoming chat as needed. Enable sending only if you want replies in chat.
3. Verify both account names before a live test. A simulated chat event is the safest first check. For an actual send, use a channel you control and explicitly enable outgoing chat.
4. If the bot cannot send, inspect the account, granted scopes and channel restrictions. EchoPaw does not ask you to make the bot a moderator for ordinary chat.

See [Twitch chat authentication](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/chat/authenticating/) and [PackGuard](/reference/packguard/).
