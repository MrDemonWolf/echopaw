---
title: Connect a separate Twitch bot
---

1. [Save the Twitch public client ID](/connect/twitch-broadcaster/). Open **Settings > Twitch** and choose **Connect bot**.
2. Choose **Open Twitch** and check that you are signing in as your bot. If your browser uses your broadcaster login, open [Twitch activation](https://www.twitch.tv/activate) in a private window or separate browser profile and enter the code shown in EchoPaw.
3. Approve the bot’s read-chat permission (`user:read:chat`). Return to EchoPaw and check **Connected as** for the bot. Each role requires a different Twitch account.

You can disconnect one role and keep the other. The bot does not need moderator access. Event subscriptions and outgoing chat are pending. [Twitch chat authentication](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/chat/authenticating/)
