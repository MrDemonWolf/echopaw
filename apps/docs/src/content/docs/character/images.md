---
title: PNG requirements
---

Use transparent, static PNGs on a shared canvas. Start around 1024 × 1024 pixels and use the same visible character placement in both images.

- **idle.png** shows your resting pose.
- **talking.png** shows the speaking pose in the same position.
- Leave transparent space around the character so effects or overlays fit inside the canvas.
- Toggle the poses in the preview. If the character jumps, re-export on an identical canvas and align the feet or another consistent anchor.

A flat solid background will remain visible in capture. Test a checkerboard or contrasting OBS scene to inspect the edges.
