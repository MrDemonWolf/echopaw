---
title: Expressions and blinking
---

The two base images are enough for speaking. Supply separate closed-eye art if you want an eye blink. EchoPaw cannot infer meaningful eye movement reliably from arbitrary PNGs.

When the optional editor is available:

1. Add closed-eye or expression art on the same canvas as the base pose.
2. Preview the expression while idle and while speaking. Keep missing states on their default fallback.
3. Adjust blink timing or turn it off. For a calmer result, use reduced motion and avoid fast flashing.
4. Assign a keyboard shortcut for frequently used expressions, or use the on-screen controls.

A custom muted pose is optional. The dashboard and status controls show functional mute independently from artwork.
