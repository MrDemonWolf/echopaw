---
title: Expressions and blinking
---

**Character > Preview blink** closes the wolf's eyes briefly. Press Shift-Command-B to blink during the sound test. Turn **Auto-blink** off in the character inspector to keep only manual blinks. Reduce Motion also pauses automatic blinking.

The closed-eye images are optional. With two images, the character talks and keeps its supplied eyes. With just one closed-eye image, only its matching mouth pose blinks. [Add blink artwork](/character/import/#add-blinking)
