# Code of conduct

EchoPaw welcomes contributors and streamers of all backgrounds and experience levels. This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and events, and when representing the project publicly.

## How we work together

- Treat people with respect. Offer specific, constructive feedback on the work.
- Respect chosen names, pronouns, accessibility needs and boundaries.
- Harassment, slurs, threats, deliberate misgendering, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing and retaliation violate this code.
- Keep private messages, recordings, personal information and off-platform identities private unless you have permission to share them.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the maintainers privately at [security@mrdemonwolf.com](mailto:security@mrdemonwolf.com) with the subject **EchoPaw conduct report**. Include relevant details and tell us how you prefer to be contacted. Maintainers limit sharing to people who need to review or respond, subject to legal or safety obligations.

Maintainers can remove harmful content, ask someone to stop, restrict participation temporarily or remove access based on the harm and pattern of behavior. If a report concerns the person who would normally handle it, request an alternate reviewer in the report. Retaliation can lead to further action.

Adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
