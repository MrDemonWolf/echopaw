# Software and service terms decision

Planning draft, 2026-11-18. Publish a plain-language software and provider notice with the release. EchoPaw is designed as downloadable local software. The proposed static site gives people documentation and a download link.

## Proposed release notice

EchoPaw is free and open source under the GNU General Public License version 3. You can obtain the license and corresponding source with the release. Optional accounts for Twitch, OpenAI and ElevenLabs remain subject to each provider’s terms, access rules and charges. A Twitch broadcaster or bot account is yours, as are the third-party API keys you add.

Use character assets and voices for which you have the necessary permissions. Review your provider plan and chosen voice before using speech on a monetized stream. ElevenLabs Free allows noncommercial use subject to its terms and attribution requirements. EchoPaw reserves Free-tier output for private noncommercial testing as a product default. Choose a plan and voice with a commercial license or obtain express authorization before streaming monetized output.

## Release review

- Provide GPL-3.0 terms and corresponding source for the distributed version. Keep the license text, source offers and required notices alongside the release.
- Review support contact, contributor content licenses, trademarks and third-party obligations. Keep usage guidance separate from the GPL rights in the source and binaries.
- Use separate terms if a future hosted service introduces obligations such as an account, paid hosting, user uploads, or commitments about service availability. Review the final distribution with the owner and counsel where needed.
