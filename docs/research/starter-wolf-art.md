# Starter wolf artwork

EchoPaw's bundled blue wolf is a public sample character. On November 20, 2026, OpenAI ImageGen generated a seated blue wolf from a text description, then edited the same illustration to supply a blink and an open mouth. The inputs came from this project. The edit used the generated idle illustration as its only image reference.

The expression changes were masked onto the idle drawing, then exported on one transparent 512 × 512 canvas. All four PNGs have identical alpha channels. The two eye states differ only within the eye region. The two mouth states differ only within the muzzle. Those regions do not overlap. This keeps the paws, ears and tail still during playback. The four poses were also inspected at 100 pixels on a light background and 280 pixels on navy.
