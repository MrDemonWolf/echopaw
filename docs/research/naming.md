# EchoPaw naming review

**Decision:** Keep EchoPaw as the working name while clearance is pending. A first-pass web search on 2026-11-18 found exact-name use in voice-interactive products. That makes the overlap more relevant than an unrelated pet storefront.

| Evidence | Relevance |
| --- | --- |
| [EchoPaw store](https://echopaw.shop/) uses EchoPaw on its storefront. | Identical wording and brand discoverability risk. A TM symbol alone does not establish registration. |
| [Amoxdigi’s Echopaw AI Robot Teddy Bear](https://amoxdigi.com/products/artificial-intelligence-ai-robot-teddy-bear-toy) is presented as a conversational toy with a companion app. | Identical wording, voice interaction and app association. The audience and distribution differ. |
| [EchoPaw voice-interactive pet robot paper](https://www.irjet.net/archives/V12/i12/IRJET-V12I1267.pdf) reports a 2025 research prototype under the same name. | Prior published exact-name voice use. The paper establishes publication, not ownership of a trademark or a commercial launch. |
| [OPPO competition results](https://hci.oppo.com/uploadfiles/2025/10/20251027165220648.pdf) list an EchoPaw location-based message platform. | Identical wording in a software project. Product availability and legal status are unverified. |

The screen used exact and spaced web queries and followed relevant primary records. It did not establish a full federal, state, or international mark result. The [USPTO clearance guide](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks) calls for searching similar marks used on related goods and services, including beyond the federal database.

## Clearance before public branding

- [ ] Use [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) for EchoPaw, Echo Paw, Echo-Paw, plural and phonetic variants. Capture live and dead records, owners, status, and listed goods/services. Use Class 9 downloadable software and related voice, entertainment and online services as starting points, while considering related offerings outside the initial classes.
- [ ] Check state records, [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/), relevant launch-country registers, macOS App Store, GitHub, product listings, common-law web use and matching domains/handles.
- [ ] Ask a trademark professional to assess the conversational product overlap, jurisdictions and risk. Clear any fallback separately. Howlkin is an earlier project codename with existing uses in [tabletop miniatures](https://www.epicsnstuffs.com/products/howlkin-3d-printed-dungeons-and-dragons-miniature-statue-model-epicsnstuffs) and [fragrance](https://mythpunkolfactive.com/collections/niche-perfumes-indie-perfumes-the-stuffkin-family).
- [ ] Keep the product display name easy to change. Use an owner-confirmed durable bundle ID, update feed path and Keychain service label before signed external builds. Defer public brand launch and avoid purchasing another domain during planning.
